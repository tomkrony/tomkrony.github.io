About

I have been building software professionally since 2019. Since 2023 I work at ECKD GmbH on business-critical software for financial services, and since July 2025 I lead the engineering team there. I take care of architecture and design decisions, code reviews and mentoring, and I own our CI/CD pipelines from build to production.

Before that I spent four years at CAD-PLAN in Frankfurt writing C#/.NET desktop applications and extensions for AutoCAD and Autodesk Inventor. My background is computational engineering: during my master's at TU Dresden I implemented finite element code in FORTRAN, C++ and Python, and I am a co-author of a journal paper from that research group.

I grew up in Kerala, India, and have lived in Germany since 2016. Outside work you will find me playing cricket or badminton.
